# Community feedback and contribution guide

Use the [GitHub issue chooser](https://github.com/mirichard/pm-tools-templates/issues/new/choose)
to submit a template request, correction, problem report, rating, or general
feedback. For questions and discussion, use
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mirichard/pm-tools-templates/discussions).
For contribution instructions, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Before submitting

Issues, comments, discussions, pull requests, and attached files are public.
Do not include personal, confidential, proprietary, security-sensitive, or
identifying information. Do not attach an unsanitized original file. There is
currently no designated restricted file-transfer channel; if a file cannot be
sanitized safely before public submission, ask for a private transfer
arrangement without attaching the file and wait for a maintainer to confirm an
approved channel.

Contributors may provide content in the format they have; maintainers assess,
convert, or retain formats as appropriate. Do not perform routine conversion
just to submit. Read the [submission handling guide](docs/community/submission-handling.md)
for file safety, access, retention, review, and escalation rules.

## What happens after submission

Maintainers triage submissions, may ask for clarification, link duplicates,
and communicate a reasoned disposition and next responsible role. Accepted
requests are not scheduled until selected in the backlog. Ratings are
operational feedback, not verified evidence of adoption or template quality.
Content is published only after review and approval of the exact verified
revision; a failed publication remains pending.

Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions. Follow
the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The repository
does not promise a response time or implementation date unless one is explicitly
approved and recorded.
