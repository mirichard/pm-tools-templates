# Stakeholder performance domain

Use this domain to identify, understand, engage, and communicate with people or groups that affect or are affected by the project.

## Scope and boundary

Stakeholder includes identification, analysis, engagement strategy, audience-focused communication, expectation management, and satisfaction measurement. Internal team development belongs in [Team](../team/); reporting mechanics belong in [Measurement](../measurement/); strategic investment decisions belong in [Planning](../planning/).
