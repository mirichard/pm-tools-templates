# TEAM PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

## When to Use

- When a team needs an evidence-based review of performance and improvement needs.
- When reviewers can agree criteria, discuss observations, and assign development actions.

## When NOT to Use

- To make employment decisions from unverified template ratings alone.
- Do not report sample values, unsupported ratings, or missing data as verified performance. See [Metric without source](../../../../../../../docs/principles/anti-patterns.md#metric-source).

## 1. Purpose
*Outline the objectives of assessing team performance during the project execution phase. Include why this assessment is important for the project's success and continuous improvement.*

---

## 2. Assessment Criteria
List the key criteria used to evaluate individual and team performance. Examples include:
- Achievement of project objectives
- Collaboration and communication
- Quality of deliverables
- Adherence to schedule and budget
- Problem-solving and innovation

---

## 3. Rating Scale
Define the rating scale that will be applied consistently across all criteria, for example:
| Rating | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| 5 | Outstanding performance |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 2 | Needs improvement |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory |

## 5. Overall Team Assessment
Provide a summary of the team's overall performance, highlighting strengths, areas for improvement, and recommended actions.

*Example: The development team consistently met sprint goals and collaborated effectively, earning an overall rating of 4. To further improve, additional cross-training is recommended to reduce single points of failure.*
