1. Prepare for the review

You can start with an existing plan. You may need to confirm estimates and availability with others before evaluating it.

Bring your plan

Identify its version, milestone, included tasks and review dates. Manual entry is supported; Excel, MPP and Smartsheet imports are not available.

Confirm work and capacity

Ask task owners for remaining person-hours. Ask resource managers for qualified hours available in the same period, after leave and competing work.

Gather readiness evidence

Check prerequisite dates with providers, interface readiness with technical owners, and skill coverage with delivery leads. Keep document or issue references.

What if my plan only has tasks and percent complete?

Percent complete does not reliably establish remaining effort. Obtain estimates for unfinished work, exclude duplicate summary rows, and document any conversion assumptions. An assignment or headcount does not establish available qualified hours.

Missing information is acceptable. Leave evidence blank when unknown. The review will explain which checks cannot be assessed and what to gather next.

3. Add supporting evidence

Blank means unknown; zero is a confirmed value. Enter only checks relevant to your selected work. Empty groups remain “Not assessed,” not “Passed.”

Evidence applies to this plan

One confirmation date and review-due date applies to all evidence in this review. Confirm every included item; split reviews if evidence cannot share these dates.

Enter the version the owners confirmed; it must match the current plan to assess checks.

When owners last verified all included evidence. Do not use today to disguise old evidence.

Capacity

Sum owner-confirmed unfinished work for this scope and period. Do not derive it from percent complete alone.

Confirmed hours available for this work after leave and other assignments; count shared people once. Zero means no capacity.

Describe calendars, allocations, skills, exclusions and estimates. Do not assume every working day equals 8 hours.

The check compares totals. It does not prove specialist availability, task sequencing or delivery feasibility.

Dependencies

Record prerequisites needed by the selected work, with owner-confirmed availability dates.

Critical interfaces

Include interfaces needed for this milestone. Verified requires supporting evidence; blocked requires a documented issue.

Required skills

Confirm qualified coverage with delivery leads; a job title alone is insufficient.
