## 🧭 Selecting a Methodology

**You need to:** Decide whether to use Traditional, Agile, or Hybrid for your project.

**Quick start:** [Methodology Selection Guide](quick-start-kits/methodology-selection-guide.md)

**Decision criteria:**

| Factor | → Traditional | → Agile | → Hybrid |
|--------|-------------|---------|----------|
| Requirements | Well-defined, stable | Evolving, unclear | Mixed |
| Change frequency | Low | High | Moderate |
| Delivery cadence | End of project | Every 1-4 weeks | Milestones + increments |
| Documentation needs | Extensive | Minimal | Variable |
| Compliance requirements | High | Low | Mixed |
| Team experience | Traditional | Agile | Mixed |

**Deep dive:** [Methodology Frameworks](methodology-frameworks/) — Detailed guides for [Agile/Scrum](methodology-frameworks/agile-scrum/), [Hybrid approaches](methodology-frameworks/hybrid-approaches/)
