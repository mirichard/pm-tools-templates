# Governance Event Notifications

Use these copy-ready patterns with the [event catalog and response process](event-driven-controls.md). Replace placeholders before sending. Use the charter's verified recipients and channels, retain delivery/acknowledgement evidence, and follow established incident procedures for security or safety events. Link restricted evidence rather than copying sensitive details into broad distribution.

## Initial alert

**Subject:** [Project] [Occurrence ID / E01–E12] [Immediate/Urgent/Routine] — [Decision or action needed]

**To:** [Named responsible owner and backup]

**Decision authority:** [Name/role]

**Detected:** [Timestamp/time zone]

**Evidence as-of:** [Timestamp/source link]

**Condition:** [Observed value/event; approved threshold, operator, window and baseline version]

**Impact:** [Affected objective, scope, gate or commitment; confirmed facts and unknowns]

**Current response:** [Authorized containment/actions, owner and interim restrictions]

**Decision/action requested:** [Specific request and authority needed]

**Acknowledge by:** [Timestamp/time zone; reply confirming ownership]

**Response plan due:** [Timestamp/time zone]

**Next update:** [Timestamp/channel]

**Linked records:** [Risk/issue/change/incident and evidence links]

**If overdue:** [Backup and next escalation authority/channel]

Scheduled reviews and mandatory gates remain in effect. This notification does not authorize a change or release.

## Escalation or decision request

**Subject:** [Project] ESCALATION [Occurrence ID / E01–E12] — [Overdue acknowledgement/plan or worsening impact]

**To:** [Next authorized decision owner; backup; original owner]

**Original detected time:** [Timestamp/time zone — retain original clock]

**Original alert:** [Link and delivery/acknowledgement history]

**Escalation reason:** [Missed deadline or new evidence; observed vs required]

**Impact and restrictions:** [Affected commitments, unresolved exposure and work on hold]

**Decision/action requested:** [Options, recommendation and required authority]

**Accountable response owner:** [Name; delegate if authorized]

**Acknowledge by:** [Timestamp/time zone under escalation policy]

**Response plan due:** [Original deadline and overdue duration; any authorized recovery deadline]

**Next update:** [Timestamp/channel]

**Linked records:** [Source event, decisions, actions and restricted evidence]

A revised recovery deadline does not erase the missed deadline. Silence is not approval; continue authorized escalation and retain affected restrictions.

## Update and closure

**Subject:** [Project] [UPDATE/CLOSED] [Occurrence ID / E01–E12] — [Outcome]

**To:** [Original recipients and accountable decision/control owner]

**Original detected time:** [Timestamp/time zone]

**Current state:** [Responding/closed; do not close on a favorable metric alone]

**Actions and evidence:** [Completed actions, verification results and source dates]

**Decision and authority:** [Decision, named approver, timestamp and approval link]

**Residual exposure:** [Remaining risks/obligations and accountable handover owner]

**Restrictions:** [Retained or explicitly released; authority and evidence]

**Follow-up / next update:** [Owner, due date and next scheduled governance review]

**Linked records:** [Final issue/change/risk status, event history and retained evidence]

If evidence is insufficient or approval is outstanding, send an update and keep the occurrence open. Closure does not cancel scheduled reviews or automatically reduce the governance tier.
