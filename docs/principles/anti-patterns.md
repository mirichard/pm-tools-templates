<a id="metadata-theater"></a>

## Metadata theater

**Domains:** Planning / Measurement

**Description:** Tags, checklists, or traceability rows are completed without changing decisions.

**Symptoms:** Many filled fields but no linked evidence, decision, or follow-up.

**Impact:** False assurance and duplicated administration hide missing work.

**Correct alternative:** Connect each recorded requirement or rating to evidence and a decision owner.

## Value equals output

**Domains:** Measurement / Stakeholder

**Description:** Delivered features, hours, or expenditure are mistaken for realized benefits.

**Symptoms:** Reports count activity while benefit owners, baselines, and outcome observations are missing.

**Impact:** Investment can continue without evidence that it improves outcomes.

**Correct alternative:** Separate forecast and realized benefits; record measures, owners, disbenefits, and review dates.

<a id="baseline-green"></a>

## Rebaseline to green

**Domains:** Planning / Measurement

**Description:** A new baseline silently replaces the original commitment to remove unfavorable variance.

**Symptoms:** Historical targets disappear or forecast revisions are reported as original plans.

**Impact:** Sponsors lose visibility of cost, schedule, and benefit underperformance.

**Correct alternative:** Retain original and approved revised baselines with change authority, reasons, and dates.

## Stakeholder broadcast

**Domains:** Stakeholder / Team

**Description:** Sending information is treated as engagement or agreement.

**Symptoms:** Updates are distributed but affected people cannot influence decisions or see feedback closure.

**Impact:** Unresolved needs, dissent, and adoption risks emerge late.

**Correct alternative:** Identify decisions requiring input; record feedback, accountable responses, and unresolved concerns.

<a id="tailoring-omission"></a>

## Tailoring by omission

**Domains:** Delivery / Uncertainty

**Description:** An essential control is removed without assessing its purpose or replacement.

**Symptoms:** Method labels are used to excuse missing acceptance, traceability, or recovery evidence.

**Impact:** Unacknowledged exposure is transferred to users or operational teams.

**Correct alternative:** Document context, risk, authority, retained safeguards, and the reason for each material tailoring decision.

<a id="maximum-governance"></a>

## Maximum governance

**Domains:** Team / Planning

**Description:** The most elaborate governance or coordination structure is applied to every initiative.

**Symptoms:** Small independent teams maintain multiple approval layers or meetings with no useful decision.

**Impact:** Decision latency and reporting overhead crowd out delivery and learning.

**Correct alternative:** Use the minimum coordination needed for team size, dependencies, and risk; retain mandatory safeguards.

<a id="metric-source"></a>

## Metric without source

**Domains:** Measurement

**Description:** A precise score or dashboard status lacks a traceable definition and observation.

**Symptoms:** Numbers have no as-of date, formula, data owner, or uncertainty; missing values become green.

**Impact:** Users make decisions on misleading comparisons or fabricated precision.

**Correct alternative:** Record definition, source, period, calculation, owner, and uncertainty; show missing data as unknown.

## Template as substitute for judgment

**Domains:** Delivery / Uncertainty

**Description:** An example or completed document is treated as tested operational capability or a decision.

**Symptoms:** No accountable person checks applicability, environment assumptions, or actual execution evidence.

**Impact:** Untested assumptions become operational failures and unresolved risks.

**Correct alternative:** Adapt to the real context, test the relevant behavior, and record the decision and its evidence.

<a id="self-approval"></a>

## Self-approval

**Domains:** Governance / Delivery

**Description:** Author completion or self-check is represented as separate authorized acceptance.

**Symptoms:** The approver is missing, lacks authority, or has no supporting review evidence.

**Impact:** Material defects and conflicts can pass without appropriate challenge.

**Correct alternative:** Separate author checks from required acceptance; name the authorized reviewer and record the decision.

<a id="lessons-archive"></a>

## Lessons archive

**Domains:** Team / Delivery

**Description:** Findings and retrospective notes are stored but do not change subsequent work.

**Symptoms:** Actions lack owners, completion evidence, or a later effectiveness check.

**Impact:** The same failure recurs and learning effort produces little improvement.

**Correct alternative:** Assign a small set of actions; check outcomes and feed useful changes into the next plan or standard.
