## What This Bundle Does NOT Cover

- **Agile ceremonies** — This is a traditional/waterfall bundle. For agile at scale, see [Scaled Agile Bundle](scaled-agile-large-bundle.md).
- **Day-to-day execution templates** — Status reports, issue logs, and change requests are assumed to be in place already.
- **Industry templates beyond healthcare and finance** — IT security templates are available in [Industry Specializations](../../../industry-specializations/information-technology/).

## Next Steps

1. **Establish the PMO structure** — Define governance roles and decision authority
2. **Complete Program and Project Management Plans** — These are your master references
3. **Initialize risk and compliance frameworks** — Set up the Enterprise Risk Assessment and applicable compliance templates
4. **Deploy executive reporting** — Dashboard and formal reports from day one
5. **Schedule governance reviews** — Monthly or bi-weekly governance checkpoints
