## What This Bundle Does NOT Cover

- **Pure agile ceremonies** — If your team is mostly agile, consider the [Standard Agile Bundle](standard-agile-medium-bundle.md) and add hybrid elements selectively.
- **Full traditional planning** — If your project requires comprehensive upfront planning, consider the [Standard Traditional Bundle](standard-traditional-medium-bundle.md).
- **Enterprise governance** — For large-scale or regulated hybrid programs, add governance templates from the [Enterprise Governance Bundle](enterprise-governance-bundle.md).
- **Industry compliance** — Add from [Industry Specializations](../../../industry-specializations/) based on your domain.

## Next Steps

1. **Define methodology boundaries** — Use the [Hybrid Infrastructure Template](../../../methodology-frameworks/hybrid/infrastructure/hybrid-infrastructure-template.md) to decide which project elements follow traditional vs. agile
2. **Set up the Integrated Change Strategy** — This is the key hybrid artifact that bridges both worlds
3. **Initialize the Risk Register** — Hybrid projects carry extra integration risk
4. **Establish reporting cadence** — Status reports and dashboard for stakeholder visibility
5. **Review and adapt in 4 weeks** — Hybrid approaches require ongoing calibration
