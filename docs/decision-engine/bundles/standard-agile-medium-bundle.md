# Standard Agile Bundle (Medium Project)

**For:** Experienced agile teams running mid-size Scrum projects
**Methodology:** Agile
**Scale:** Medium (10–50 people, 3–12 months)
**Risk Level:** Medium
**Rules Trace:** R002, R005, R008, R017, R021, R024

## What This Bundle Does NOT Cover

- **SAFe/LeSS scaling** — Medium teams don't need scaling frameworks. For 50+ people, see the [Scaled Agile Bundle](scaled-agile-large-bundle.md).
- **Executive governance** — No executive dashboards or program management. Add these from the [Business Stakeholder Suite](../../../business-stakeholder-suite/) if needed.
- **Industry compliance** — Add industry templates from [Industry Specializations](../../../industry-specializations/) based on your domain.

## Next Steps

1. **Set up the Product Backlog** with your Product Owner
2. **Create the Release Plan** for the next 2–3 sprints
3. **Initialize the Risk Register** with known risks
4. **Run your first sprint** using the Sprint Planning template
5. **Review and adapt** after Sprint 2 using the retrospective
