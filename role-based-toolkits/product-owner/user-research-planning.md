# User Research Planning Template

Use this plan before recruiting participants. Connect the research to a product decision and record what the study can and cannot establish. A small exploratory study can identify problems; it does not establish population-wide prevalence.

## Decision and scope

- **Product / feature:** [Name and version]
- **Research owner:** [Name]
- **Decision owner:** [Name]
- **Decision to inform:** [Specific choice and decision date]
- **Existing evidence:** [Analytics, support feedback, earlier studies]
- **Unknowns:** [What the existing evidence cannot answer]
- **Out of scope:** [Excluded users, features, or questions]

| Research question | Assumption to test | Evidence needed | Decision affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Question] | [Assumption] | [Observed behavior / interview evidence] | [Choice] |

## Participants and method

Choose interviews for experiences and motivations, observation for actual work, or task-based usability sessions for findability and completion. Explain why the method answers the question.

- **Method and rationale:** [Method, setting, limitations]
- **Participant groups:** [Roles, experience, accessibility needs]
- **Recruitment criteria:** [Inclusion / exclusion criteria]
- **Target participation and rationale:** [Number and why it is adequate for this study]
- **Repository or product familiarity:** [Record for each participant]
- **Recruitment owner and schedule:** [Owner / dates]

## Session plan

Use neutral prompts that do not reveal the expected route or answer. Pilot the script before the study and record changes to it.

| Task or prompt | Starting state | Success criteria | Observation to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| [User goal in participant language] | [Environment and starting screen] | [Observable outcome] | [Route, hesitation, errors, assistance, time] |

- **Facilitator / observer:** [Names]
- **Consent and recording arrangements:** [Participant agreement and storage arrangements]
- **Data handling:** [Minimum information needed, authorized access, deletion date]
- **Stopping conditions:** [Participant withdrawal, distress, technical failure]

## Findings and decisions

Separate observation from interpretation. Include unsuccessful attempts and contradictory evidence. Do not infer general usability from owner-only testing.

| Observation | Participant context | Interpretation | Confidence and limitation | Action / owner / due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [What happened] | [Relevant experience; use participant code] | [Proposed explanation] | [Evidence strength and missing coverage] | [Action] |

- **Decision:** [Proceed / revise / further research, with rationale]
- **Unanswered questions:** [Remaining uncertainty]
- **Follow-up validation:** [What will be tested after changes]
- **Decision recorded by / date:** [Name / date]
