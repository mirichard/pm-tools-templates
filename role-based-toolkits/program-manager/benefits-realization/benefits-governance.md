# Benefits Governance

**Governance tiers:** Standard, Rigorous.

Apply the [adaptive governance model](../../../docs/governance/governance-decision-matrix.md) to select required evidence, authorities and cadence. Tailor sample structures below to the approved charter; retain mandatory controls.

Cadence & Controls:
- Gate Reviews validate benefit assumptions and realization evidence
- Quarterly Benefits Review adjusts targets and investment mix
- Post-Implementation Review verifies benefits and records variances

Artifacts:
- Updated Benefits Register and KPI Tracker
- Executive summary for Steering Committee
