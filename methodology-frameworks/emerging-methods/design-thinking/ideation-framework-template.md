## Ideation Techniques

### Technique 1: Brainstorming (30 minutes)

**Rules for Brainstorming:**
- Defer judgment - no criticism during generation
- Strive for quantity - aim for 50+ ideas
- Build on ideas - use "Yes, and..." approach
- Encourage wild ideas - think outside the box
- Stay focused - stick to the HMW statements
- Be visual - use sketches and diagrams

## Idea Evaluation Framework

### Evaluation Criteria

**Primary Criteria:**
1. **Desirability** (User/Market Need)
   - Addresses real user pain point
   - Clear value proposition
   - Market demand exists

2. **Feasibility** (Technical/Operational)
   - Technically possible to implement
   - Resources and skills available
   - Timeline realistic

3. **Viability** (Business/Financial)
   - Financially sustainable
   - Aligns with business strategy
   - ROI potential

**Secondary Criteria:**
- Innovation level
- Implementation complexity
- Risk level
- Scalability potential
- Competitive advantage
