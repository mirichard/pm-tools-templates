# Sponsor-readiness review rubric v1.0

Status: proposed research instrument, not a validated standard. Apply to the case's explicit approval criteria; do not impose software-development assumptions or infer universal sponsor rules.

## Criterion-level assessment

For each criterion, record one status: supported; unmet; not assessable; not applicable with justification. Cite evidence and distinguish direct statements from inference. An unsupported absence claim is not a demonstrated failure.

Independent adjudicators use a criterion/finding reference register prepared before model outputs. New valid concerns can be added through blinded adjudication with a versioned rationale.

## Quality dimensions

Score each dimension separately; do not sum into a score that hides a critical error.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence fidelity | Fabricated, wrong or unsupported | Relevant but incomplete | Correct locations support the claim and qualification |
| Coverage | Material criterion omitted | Mentioned without adequate assessment | Applicable criterion assessed with evidence or explicit inability to assess |
| Decision rationale | Contradicts approval rules or ignores blockers | Plausible but incompletely explains consequence | Recommendation follows rules and explains blockers, conditions and uncertainty |
| Follow-up usefulness | Generic, unnecessary or misleading | Relevant but incomplete | Role, action, requested evidence and decision deadline are explicit |
| Uncertainty handling | Missing evidence treated as failure or certainty invented | Caveat present but unclear | Fact, inference, missing evidence and residual uncertainty distinguished |

Mark severity independently:
- Critical: would cause an unjustified authorization or hide a criterion explicitly required before authorization.
- Material: could change decision conditions, scope, cost, staffing, timing or confidence in the proposed method.
- Minor: useful correction without a material decision consequence.

Do not award a finding for length, repeated wording, agreement with another model, or repeating information without relating it to the decision.

## Measures

- Criterion coverage: adequately assessed applicable criteria / all applicable criteria. Report supported/unmet/not-assessable counts separately.
- Material concern recall: valid material reference concerns identified / material reference concerns.
- Unsupported burden: unsupported or overstated concerns and minutes spent investigating them.
- Evidence fidelity: supported consequential claims / all consequential claims.
- Decision quality: dimension scores, critical errors and adjudicator explanation; no automatic pass from a total score.
- Human effort: source preparation + review + verification + correction + decision/disposition minutes. Keep research curation separate.
- Brief usability: whether the PM can locate the recommendation, basis, unresolved decisions and next actions without re-reading the entire analysis; record observed difficulties.
- Report every denominator; zero denominator means not estimable. Present per-case results before aggregate comparisons.

No numerical success threshold is approved. A constructed case can test workflow and rule adherence, not establish real-world performance or PM time savings.
