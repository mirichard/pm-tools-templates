## Disposition

SYN-APP-01 is suitable as a development exercise after its ambiguities are resolved. It is not ready as evidence of AI value. Its obvious schedule conflict and capacity subtraction could be handled by ordinary PM review or a checklist. Successful answers would establish basic mechanics only.

Retain the three comparison conditions and the distinction between research curation and operational PM effort. Do not replace the value question with defect-detection accuracy.

## Operational addendum for the pilot

These controls supplement protocol v1.0; they do not change source evidence or certify readiness.

**Recommendation:** “not ready” means a known mandatory precondition is unmet now. “Insufficient evidence” means a mandatory precondition cannot be determined and no known mandatory failure already determines the recommendation. “Ready with conditions” requires all mandatory preconditions supported and every permitted condition to identify owner, closure evidence and deadline before affected work. “Ready” requires all applicable criteria supported. Record missing evidence even when a known failure already determines “not ready.”

**Compound criteria:** record obligation-level supported/unmet/not-assessable/not-applicable statuses. Roll up to unmet if any applicable obligation is demonstrably unmet; otherwise not assessable if any material applicable obligation lacks evidence; otherwise supported. Use not applicable only with a case-specific rationale. A6 being partly documented is not proof that no risk management exists.

**Scoring:** independent reference register columns are concern ID, criterion/obligation, exact source location, fact/inference, severity, acceptable alternative interpretation, decision effect and required follow-up. Count a concern once even if it affects several criteria. Count applicable criteria once for coverage. Record reference-absent valid concerns separately until adjudication. Score fidelity and uncertainty per consequential claim, coverage per criterion, usefulness per action and rationale per final brief. Preserve individual scores and disagreements rather than masking them with an average.

**Effort:** record non-overlapping intervals for preparation, initial review, verification, correction and final disposition. Record active human minutes and elapsed time separately. Include source gathering/conversion, prompt preparation, retries, checking rejected findings and final editing in operational effort where performed by the PM. Research-only de-identification, reference labeling and study administration remain separate. A preassembled packet study measures review-stage effort only; it cannot establish end-to-end savings from a PM's original records.

**Allocation and tools:** provide the same frozen evidence, task and output format to all conditions. A/B use the same allowed non-AI tools; only C receives AI. Freeze model/configuration, prompt and permitted follow-up interactions before evaluation; log all calls, failures and raw drafts. Balance reviewer experience and case difficulty across conditions; a participant must not see the same case or related variants twice. Record deviations and exposure to answers. This public development case and its curator notes cannot be treated as a secure held-out test.
