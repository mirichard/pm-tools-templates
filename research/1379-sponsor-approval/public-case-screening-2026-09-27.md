## Selection and admission gate

Select **Haringey for further qualification of a bounded public-record task**, not for the comparative study. Its contemporaneous Cabinet recommendations and staged OBC-to-FBC decision can anchor a realistic decision; the exempt financing is a material limit. The precise task would ask for a PM recommendation based on the public packet *as of 12/07/2021*, recording nonassessable matters rather than judging the Council's actual vote. It cannot claim to reproduce the complete Cabinet evidence set. Newport remains an alternate if its annex and actual approval record become accessible.

Before admission: (1) retrieve all public item-682 attachments and verify exact versions, page ranges, bytes/hashes and readable graphics; (2) map each applicable contemporary recommendation/governance criterion to public evidence and mark exempt gaps, especially financing, contract terms and sensitivities; (3) have independent PM reviewer determine whether that limited task is realistic despite withholding; (4) verify applicable permissions for controlled participant access and model processing, with publisher links or permission instead of redistribution; (5) establish independent reference judgments on the frozen, accessible subset. If exempt evidence is essential to the decision, **do not admit** the case without it; switch case or record a gap.
