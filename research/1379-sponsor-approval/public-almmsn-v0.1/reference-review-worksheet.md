## Obligation records

Create a separate row for each assessable obligation under R1–R10. Split R9 by applicable requirement and by status, authority and completion timing. Preserve evaluation factors as factors, not absolute pass/fail conditions.

| Record ID | Requirement and obligation | Applicability and rationale | Evidence status | Source/page and quoted or paraphrased evidence | Fact / interpretation / gap | Decision effect and materiality | Acceptable alternative interpretation | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| To assign | To assess | Pending | Unscored | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending | Pending |

“Unscored” is a workflow placeholder, not a fifth evidence status. Completed records use Supported, Demonstrably unmet, Not assessable from supplied evidence, or Not applicable with a case-specific rationale.

Any applicable demonstrably unmet obligation makes the grouped criterion unmet; otherwise a material evidence gap makes it not assessable; otherwise supported. A wholly inapplicable group needs justified exclusions for every obligation. Missing documentation is not proof that an activity was not performed. An unfavorable evaluation factor can still be adequately assessed; record its decision effect separately.

## Proposed disagreement procedure

1. Reviewers complete and preserve their records independently before discussion and before seeing any comparison outputs.
2. Compare applicability, evidence status, materiality, concern matches and decision reasoning. Record each disagreement and its source basis.
3. An independent adjudicator records resolution and rationale, accepted alternative interpretations, or an unresolved/unscorable item. Preserve original judgments; do not force agreement.
4. Freeze the reference version, applicable-obligation list, material-concern list and exclusions before evaluation. Report both counts and exclusion reasons.
5. If an output raises a potentially valid new concern, remove condition identifiers before adjudication. Record the reference amendment and rescore every affected output consistently; report original and amended results. Never silently change the denominator.
