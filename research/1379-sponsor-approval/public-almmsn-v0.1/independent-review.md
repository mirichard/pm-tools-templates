# PUB-ALMMSN-01 independent case review v0.1

For a willing experienced PM, with domain advice where needed. No reviewer is assigned by this document. This is a case-suitability review, not a comparative trial.

## Materials

Start with [source-pack.md](source-pack.md) and [approval-requirements.md](approval-requirements.md). Record an initial view before reading [checklist.md](checklist.md). Do not read curator notes, the qualification report, issue/PR discussion, the final 2024 decision or subsequent results before forming independent judgments. Disclose any earlier exposure; do not claim blindness after exposure.

## Requested review

1. Is this a realistic PM decision at the stated public-proposal stage? Is readiness for sponsor consideration distinguishable from readiness to start work?
2. Do the sources support a defensible recommendation with explicit limits? Identify any missing material that would prevent the intended assessment.
3. Are the historical requirements accurate, proportionate and separated into assessable obligations? Identify mandatory conditions versus comparative factors.
4. Is the 67-page selection sufficient and manageable? Identify required additions and removable material by source/page and decision consequence.
5. Could ordinary PM practice and a checklist reasonably assess the case? Identify specialist knowledge, obvious-answer effects or source-author recommendations that could distort the comparison.
6. After recording that view, review the B/C checklist for omissions, leading wording and unequal information.
