# PUB-ALMMSN-01 approval requirements v0.1

Common material for all proposed comparison conditions. These are author paraphrases with source locators, pending independent confirmation. They describe the historical criteria; they are not a mandated review sequence. S1/S2 refer to [source-pack.md](source-pack.md).

## Historical evaluation factors

S1 §3 incorporates the evaluation process in S2 §3.0. These factors require a reasoned comparison, not six binary prerequisites. In particular, benefiting only one resource does not automatically disqualify an alternative. No weights or acceptance thresholds have been invented.

Do not treat every listed statute as applicable automatically. Assess R9 separately for each applicable requirement; source status, completion timing and consequence need separate evidence. Do not substitute today's rules.

The review also needs scope, schedule, resources and dependencies to assess the factors above. These are evidence questions, not an invented requirement to use a particular planning tool, name every staff member or adopt SYN-APP-01's fictional sponsor rules.

## Reporting semantics

For each obligation record **Supported**, **Demonstrably unmet**, **Not assessable from supplied evidence**, or **Not applicable** with a case-specific rationale. Separate a documented assertion from verified support. Record source/page, rationale, materiality and follow-up.

For grouped obligations: any applicable demonstrably unmet obligation makes the group unmet; otherwise a material evidence gap makes it not assessable; otherwise supported. Report a wholly inapplicable group only when every obligation has a justified exclusion.

This roll-up describes evidence coverage and obligation compliance. It does not determine sponsor approval mechanically. Evaluation factors may be adequately assessed despite an unfavorable finding. State the unfavorable finding separately and explain its decision effect. Recommendations may include conditions only with a source-supported basis for what can remain pending at the selected stage; otherwise request that authority.
