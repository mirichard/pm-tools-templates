# PUB-ALMMSN-01 fixed checklist v0.1

Proposed B/C condition material only. Do not distribute to normal-review condition A. All conditions receive the same sources and historical approval requirements. This checklist remains subject to independent review and freeze.

1. Confirm the proposed decision, evidence date, approval authority and applicable requirements.
2. Map each material obligation to source evidence. Separate author assertions, corroboration, demonstrated failures and unknowns.
3. Check scope, schedule, resource assumptions, costs and dependencies where they affect the decision; verify calculations against source quantities.
4. Establish which unresolved items must precede approval and which may be conditions. Do not infer authority to waive requirements.
5. Verify material findings and specify the evidence, responsible role and latest safe decision point for follow-up. Do not invent named owners or deadlines.
6. Provide the PM recommendation with its limits and record preparation, review, verification, corrections and follow-up effort.

C uses this identical checklist plus the separately frozen AI instruction. This document does not authorize AI execution, select a model, set a benefit threshold or supply expected findings.
