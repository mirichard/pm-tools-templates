## Completeness and suitability judgment

**Why advance this case:** there is a real funding decision, an unaltered predecision proposal, explicit approval criteria, accessible supporting records and a manageable main document. The task can ask a PM to identify what is supported, what still requires verification and what conditions or further evidence the sponsor needs. No defects have been added.

**Limits requiring independent review:**

- The draft is an earlier public snapshot. It does not reproduce every internal record available in July. Study claims must be about readiness assessment from this supplied snapshot, not whether the historical approval was correct.
- Detailed staffing commitments, underlying cost calculations and independent performance evidence are not established merely by narrative assurances. Record absent support as unknown, not proof of nonperformance.
- The baseline is large and incorporates further sources. Freeze the exact relevant excerpts and trace their references before admitting a participant packet. If a consequential judgment depends on missing evidence, obtain it or explicitly limit that judgment. Do not silently discard a material requirement to shorten the exercise.
- Ecological expertise may affect effort and judgment. An experienced PM must assess whether the task tests PM review reasonably; record specialist help and count its effort equally across conditions.
- Public author evaluations create anchoring risk. Preserve authentic recommendations, but do not treat them as independent truth. Independent reference judgments remain necessary.

Preparation burden is moderate for the main proposal, potentially high for reference tracing. It has not been timed. Count sourcing, extraction, verification, corrections and follow-up separately; a supplied-packet experiment alone cannot establish end-to-end savings.
