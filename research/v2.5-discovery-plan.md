## Promotion gates

**Research → backlog candidate:** problem/user evidence; existing-work inventory; bounded proposed outcome and exclusions; options and feasibility; dependencies/risks; recommendation; linked evidence and recorded owner disposition. Retain the research item and link a delivery candidate; do not move the whole epic simply because one slice is viable.

**Backlog candidate → sprint ready:** named owner; leaf-sized scope; testable acceptance criteria and validation method; dependencies resolved or explicitly sequenced; estimate; recorded owner readiness decision. Actual sprint selection additionally requires a sprint goal and confirmed capacity after research allocation. A checked checklist or merged PR alone does not satisfy acceptance.

**At each checkpoint:** update each covered epic's Discovery stage and next action, create/link bounded child candidates for viable slices, record defer/stop reasons, review WIP and confirm the next investigation owners. Keep uninvestigated items in Intake/Prioritized; do not label them Discovery in progress merely because they appear in this plan.
