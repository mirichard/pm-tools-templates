# Condition B: fixed checklist comparator v1.0

Freeze this version before development runs. Use the same source packet, context access, task boundary and recording form as conditions A and C. No AI assistance in B. Ordinary PDF search, notes, calculators and existing non-AI tools are permitted; record tools used.

Complete the following steps, recording only decision-relevant findings.

1. **Inventory the evidence.** Note each document's author, stated version, approval date and scope. Distinguish publication, approval, revision and effective dates. Record uncertainty.
2. **Extract explicit commitments.** For each relevant obligation, record deliverable/activity, responsible role, action, date or relative timing, prerequisite and source location. Preserve qualifiers and exceptions.
3. **Match like with like.** Compare the same deliverable, population, unit, stage and applicable period. A revision or different scope may explain a difference.
4. **Check compatibility.** Compare dates, quantities, roles, approval order and explicit prerequisites. Arithmetic can identify differences but cannot establish that two obligations have the same meaning or authority.
5. **Check authority.** Look for supersession, exceptions and approval evidence. When authority is unresolved, ask a bounded clarification; do not assert conflict.
6. **Verify every finding.** Reopen both source locations, including figure labels and footnotes. Classify C supported conflict; S consistent; V valid revision/different scope; U unresolved. Remove duplicate alerts.
7. **Specify a useful action.** Identify the responsible role, exact clarification or reconciliation required, evidence needed and possible effect on the selected decision. Do not invent dates or quantify general project risk.
8. **Record disposition and effort.** Capture accepted/clarified/rejected/unresolved, all investigation time and incomplete work. Report no supported conflict if that is the evidence-based result.

## Deterministic rules

- Different values trigger comparison, not an automatic conflict.
- A prerequisite later than its dependent activity is a candidate concern only when the dependency, scope and applicable dates are explicit.
- A missing value produces an information-gap record, not an assumed value.
- Ambiguous units, working calendars or relative-date anchors remain unresolved until established.
- A superseded obligation must not be treated as current without supporting authority.

This is a substantive manual comparator. Do not weaken it after seeing AI results.
