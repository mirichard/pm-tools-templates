## Decision brief

**Recommendation — revise, do not implement sentiment analysis now.** Reuse the maintained stakeholder register and assess a small, standalone improvement for recording explicit concerns, their source/confirmation, owner, response and review date. Keep the register useful without any automated analysis. Treat optional sentiment/stance tagging as a separate, conditional research path: its value over manual concern tracking is not established.

The repository contains maintained register assets, but no located maintained sentiment model, evaluation set, or implementation matching the historical prototype claims. The issue's WorldCIST citation points to a Springer chapter DOI, but the chapter, DOI metadata, and other official sources could not be retrieved in this environment. The September “GO” comments are historical; the current issue and roadmap explicitly say unscheduled and uncommitted.

**Owner decision requested:** retain/revise/defer the manual register slice; decide whether to preserve #92 solely as a future evaluation candidate; and explicitly retire the old numerical targets or authorize a separately scoped, permissioned evaluation plan. ## Data, interpretation, and human-review safeguards

- **Consent and authority:** Before any later use of real communications, identify the purpose, controller/owner, lawful authority and applicable notice/consent requirements, access, retention/deletion, and permitted sources. Consent is not presumed from a stakeholder relationship, public availability, project participation, or message delivery. No communication data were collected for this report.
- **Minimize and separate:** Prefer manually entered, stakeholder-confirmed concerns. If a later evaluation is authorized, use the minimum necessary text and context, with identifiers removed where feasible; do not create persistent person-level sentiment profiles or use tags for employment, performance, or other consequential judgments.
- **Context and cultural bias:** A sentence's tone can vary with language, translation, culture, role, power dynamics, politeness conventions, and local project context. Do not infer disengagement from message frequency, brevity, silence, or a single negative phrase. Report tested context/locale limits; do not generalize beyond them.
- **Abstain:** Return “uncertain / needs context” rather than a polarity, stance, or alert for sarcasm, mixed statements, negation, idiom, unknown language, missing context, or model disagreement. A missing tag is not evidence of agreement or lack of concern.
- **Human review:** Treat any automated label as a fallible text-level suggestion, preserve the quoted evidence and uncertainty, and require a reviewer to confirm, correct, or reject it before any action. A stakeholder's stated concern and requested action take precedence over a model's inferred tone.
- **No autonomous escalation:** No alerts, outreach, recommended interventions, or engagement scoring are authorized by this research. Escalations require human confirmation and ordinary project decision-making.
