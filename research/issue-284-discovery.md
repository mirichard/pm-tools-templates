## Decision brief

**Recommendation:** Keep #284 open as a roll-up epic. Treat only #103’s first-template journey as selected for v2.5.0 Sprint 5 refinement/delivery planning. Reuse #1561 (definition) and #1562 (implementation/validation); do not create a duplicate story or prioritize a broader extension yet. Retain the useful existing guides, browser editor, and examples, but do not describe them as an already integrated interactive onboarding system. Defer multi-template walkthroughs, a progressive-complexity engine, telemetry, and dashboard claims unless Michael explicitly selects them after a user outcome and measurement method are established.

**Evidence:** The maintained getting-started guide already offers a short first-template path; v2.4.0 includes five browser editors with Markdown export, local draft/version behavior, accessibility fixtures, and separate accepted intake validation. Tutorials and progression material also exist, but several tutorial pages are explicitly drafts. The browser editor’s completion percentage measures required fields, not tutorial completion. The legacy onboarding CLI describes AI and analytics capabilities that its code does not establish.

**Uncertainty:** Repository artifacts and automated checks do not establish that a first-time user can complete the journey in under ten minutes, finds it understandable, or achieves the historical satisfaction/adoption targets. No first-time-user study or target measurement was performed or authorized here.

**Owner decision:** Michael should record the disposition and measurement method (or explicit deferral) for every historical functional and outcome target before #1562 begins. Preserve the sanitized-file/text-only v2.4 boundary and the completed #1482 acceptance. No separate extension has sufficient user evidence in this research to recommend.
