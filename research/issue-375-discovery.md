## Decision brief

**Recommendation:** Revise the broad “analytics engine” into a source-linked, deterministic project-checkpoint exception review. At a scheduled checkpoint, help a PM decide whether an open, high/critical risk threatens an approved milestone enough to warrant human review or escalation. Show the risk, its response owner/deadline, the dated source, and any schedule variance; do not generate a universal health score or automatically escalate.

**Evidence:** The maintained [Project Health Assessment Template](https://github.com/mirichard/pm-tools-templates/blob/8078fdd24db8e9d274a5922c1db00c051f708663/domains/measurement/project-assessment-suite/project-health-assessment-template.md) already asks for period, baseline, source, owner and calculation, and says missing values are unknown. It also retains fixed health bands and a weighted score that can average away critical risk. The maintained [risk register](https://github.com/mirichard/pm-tools-templates/blob/8078fdd24db8e9d274a5922c1db00c051f708663/domains/uncertainty/project-lifecycle/02-planning/risk-management/risk-register-template.md) and [schedule template](https://github.com/mirichard/pm-tools-templates/blob/8078fdd24db8e9d274a5922c1db00c051f708663/domains/delivery/templates/traditional/Traditional/Process_Groups/Planning/project_schedule_template.md) contain relevant inputs, not an integrated or validated decision service. #368/#369 research is active; use #369 to settle any overlapping report contract.

**Uncertainty:** No user-validated decision threshold, source contract, representative project history, model evaluation, integrated backend, or confirmed capacity was found. The local dashboard snapshot is issue-label arithmetic, not project performance. Predictive scope remains separate from #105 and the #1329 recovery gates.

**Owner decision:** Select, revise, or defer the deterministic slice; approve its source fields, freshness rule and local escalation authority. Keep predictive features unselected unless separately evaluated and authorized.

## Method comparison and data contract

| Approach | Decision output and units | Evidence, freshness, missing data | Baseline and evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transparent descriptive rule (recommended first)** | For one checkpoint, show risk IDs/status/ordinal priority, owner and due date, and—only when an approved schedule baseline and forecast exist—their date difference in explicitly chosen **calendar days or working days**. Report counts as item counts. No single cross-domain score. | Each value needs source ID/link, source revision or snapshot, `as_of` and retrieval time, reporting period, owner, and calculation/rule version. Freshness is measured against a project-approved cadence, not a repo-wide “real-time” promise. Missing/stale/conflicting values are **unknown / conflict**, never zero, green, or inferred complete. If no approved baseline exists, withhold variance. | Compare dates only to the approved, versioned baseline; preserve it across changes. A trend requires comparable dated snapshots and stable definitions. Test deterministic outputs with synthetic cases and independent arithmetic; human owner approves thresholds, authority, and interpretation. |
| **Predictive risk / schedule forecast (deferred)** | Risk: probability of a defined event by a named horizon (0–1 or %); schedule: forecast milestone date/quantiles in a declared project calendar. A point-date error is in days; a probability score is not “percent accurate.” | Needs point-in-time, provenance-preserving project snapshots, labels/outcomes, prediction horizons, permission/privacy review, missingness and selection analysis, and representative independent cases. Leakage control and forecast freshness are essential. No qualifying history or approved target was evidenced for #375. | Compare with an agreed simple baseline (event prevalence/rule for risks; current approved deterministic/naïve method for schedules) using temporal out-of-sample evaluation. For probabilistic risks, assess calibration and a proper probability score; for dates, use a horizon-matched error measure and benchmark. Report sample sizes/uncertainty and subgroup limitations; no “80% accuracy” headline without an owner-approved metric and acceptance threshold. |

## Synthetic worked example — proposed fixture, not project data

**Question:** At the October 30 checkpoint, should the PM review a risk threatening a milestone before the next report?

| Field | Synthetic value |
|---|---|
| Checkpoint / source freshness | `2026-10-30`; risk/schedule snapshot last updated `2026-10-29T16:00Z` (20 hours earlier). Whether that is “fresh” depends on the project's approved cadence. |
| Approved schedule | Baseline `BL-2`, approved `2026-09-30`; milestone target `2026-11-06`. |
| Current forecast | `2026-11-12`; proposed difference is **+6 calendar days** (not working days). Do not overwrite `BL-2`. |
| Synthetic risk | `R-17`, open/identified; probability High (rank 3), impact High (rank 3), mitigation response due `2026-10-29`, owner missing. Under the Agile Risk Board 3×3 matrix this is Critical; `3 × 3 = 9` is an **ordinal priority score**, not a 90% probability or monetary loss. |

**Proposed rule / result:** Display one **“PM review”** exception because the supplied example has a critical open risk, no response owner, an overdue response date, and a forecast six calendar days after the approved milestone baseline. The output cites both source rows and says why it matched. It does **not** decide who to notify, change the baseline, infer causal impact, or mark the project red/green. This fixture demonstrates arithmetic and explainability only; the rule and any escalation threshold require owner approval.

**Proposed edge fixtures:** no schedule baseline → no date variance; no forecast → unknown, not zero; stale source under the locally approved cadence → stale/unknown; two conflicting forecasts → surface both with a conflict, do not choose one; missing owner → display missing owner, do not discard risk; no open risks → report zero only if the input snapshot is complete and its scope is known. A trend fixture requires two dated, definition-compatible snapshots.
