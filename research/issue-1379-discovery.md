## Proposed validation / evidence fixture — not executed

Use the worksheet’s blank obligation row to test the review and adjudication workflow without inventing Alabama findings:

1. Provide reviewers the same frozen source excerpt and one identified obligation; require each to record applicability, status, source/page, fact versus interpretation, materiality, alternative reading and follow-up independently.
2. **Proposed expected handling:** if the supplied excerpt does not establish a material fact, record “Not assessable from supplied evidence,” not “Demonstrably unmet”; do not infer that an omitted record never existed. For a compound requirement, split the obligations before scoring. If two differently worded findings describe the same underlying issue and decision consequence, link them to one concern ID rather than inflate the reference denominator.
3. Preserve both initial judgments. A named independent adjudicator records evidence, resolution or unresolved status, rationale, and the exact reference version/denominator before seeing condition outputs. If the denominator is zero, report the measure as not estimable.

This is a proposed mechanics fixture derived from the blank worksheet and v1.1 rubric. No fixture run, human judgment, agreement statistic, reference answer, or acceptance is claimed. Independent reviewers and the owner must validate the rubric and procedure before it becomes a test.
